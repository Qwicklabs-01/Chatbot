Privacy Policy for Aura AI

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Aura AI. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our mobile application ("App").

2. Data Collection and Usage

Aura AI is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal user data, accounts, or telemetry.

  • Chat and Prompts: When you send a message or use the AI tools, the text is sent securely to the Google Gemini API for processing. It is not stored on our servers.
  • Files and PDFs: Any files (PDFs, text files, images) you upload into the App are processed entirely on your local device (client-side) or sent ephemerally to the AI provider for analysis. We do not store your files.

3. Third-Party Services

This App utilizes the Google Gemini API to generate responses. By using this App, your prompts are subject to Google's API Privacy Policies.

4. Data Storage

All chat history and application settings are stored locally on your own device using browser LocalStorage. If you clear your app data or uninstall the App, this history is permanently deleted. We have no access to this data.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect email addresses, any updates will simply be reflected on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the App Store contact details.
