# Aura AI - SMM & SMO Marketing Strategy Guide

This guide outlines the Social Media Optimization (SMO) implementation details and a step-by-step Social Media Marketing (SMM) campaign plan to promote Aura AI across social platforms.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Core Value Propositions

To market Aura AI effectively, emphasize these three key pillars:
1. **Absolute Privacy (100% Client-Side)**: No user inputs ever leave the browser. Zero server data collection, zero APIs, zero account registration.
2. **Speed & Offline Access**: Instantly runs from caches and works fully in flight mode or remote areas with no network connection.
3. **All-in-One Utility Chassis**: Combines voice output, scientific formula solvers, document manipulation (PDF merging/extraction), and text paraphrasers/detectors in a single mobile chassis.

**Brand Voice**: Futurist, clean, secure, highly functional, and developer-friendly.

## 2. Social Media Optimization (SMO) Setup

We have integrated SMO metadata directly into [index.html](file:///c:/Users/Sakshi/Downloads/chatbot/index.html). When the website link is shared on platforms (e.g., Discord, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Slack), it automatically renders a gorgeous rich preview:

- **Open Graph (OG) Metadata**:
  - Title: `Aura AI - Local Mobile Assistant & Private Chatbot`
  - Image Preview: `https://aura-ai.pages.dev/assets/aura-og-preview.png` (displays a mock-up of the mobile app)
  - Description: `Access private, high-speed, client-side AI chat, offline calculators, PDF watermarking, merging, text extractors, and writing tools entirely inside your browser.`
- **Twitter Cards**:
  - Formatted as `summary_large_image` to capture the maximum timeline height on X.
- **Search Engine Schema**:
  - Included a standard JSON-LD `WebApplication` schema block to help search bots recognize Aura as a productivity software application.

## 3. Social Media Marketing (SMM) Platform Strategy

### A. Twitter (X) & LinkedIn (Professional Tech Audiences)
- **Target Audience**: Developers, privacy advocates, open-source enthusiasts, remote workers, students.
- **Content Pillar**: Tech demonstrations (GIFs/Short Videos showing offline math solving and PDF text extraction).
- **Post Template**:
  > 🔒 What if you could use an AI assistant with zero server requests and absolute privacy?
  > 
  > Aura AI runs 100% client-side in your browser. Solve equations, merge PDFs, detect AI text, and chat offline.
  > 
  > ⚡ Try it now (No signup, no costs): https://aura-ai.pages.dev/
  > 
  > #PrivacyFirst #WebDev #JavaScript #BuildInPublic

### B. TikTok & Instagram Reels (Visual / Gen-Z Study Hacks)
- **Target Audience**: Students, office workers, content creators looking for free/private tools.
- **Content Concept**: "3 Web Apps That Feel Illegal to Know." Show Aura running in flight mode (airplane mode) on a phone, calculating BMR/GPA and merging PDFs instantly.
- **Hook**: *"Stop paying for PDF merges and grammar checkers. This app does it locally inside your browser on flight mode."*

### C. Reddit & Tech Communities
- **Subreddits**: `r/selfhosted`, `r/privacy`, `r/webdev`, `r/usefulwebsites`.
- **Strategy**: Write an authentic, developer-to-developer post about building a fully functional mobile-optimized helper tool using native JavaScript, HTML5, and offline libraries without a heavy backend.
